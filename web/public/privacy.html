Privacy

Last updated 4 November 2026.

assay doesn't have accounts, doesn't set cookies, and doesn't collect personal information. This page lists what does happen when you use it, by SHYGUY LLC ("we").

The Site

  • Hosting. The Site runs on Cloudflare, which processes your IP address and request details to serve and protect it, under Cloudflare's privacy policy. Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views without cookies.
  • Fonts. Pages load their fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when you open a page (Google's privacy policy).
  • Your browser stores nothing from the Site.

The overlay (userscript and extension)

  • It sends assay.meex.work the mint addresses of the coins on the pump.fun page you're viewing, and nothing else: not your wallet, not your browsing history, not the page's content.
  • It stores two settings in your browser (whether badges are shown, and whether the panel is collapsed). They never leave your browser.
  • Its requests reach us through Cloudflare, as above. We don't log which mints a given visitor asked about.

The Telegram channel

Assay Office posts on Telegram, and its discussion group is read-only. If you follow it or react to a post, Telegram processes that under Telegram's privacy policy. Our bot only reads the channel's own posts.

What assay records

assay records public data: pump.fun launches and trades from the Solana blockchain (wallet addresses included, as they appear on-chain), coins' public metadata and links, and its own findings. It does not try to link wallets to people.
