What assay is

assay reads public data (the Solana blockchain, and the metadata and links pump.fun coins publish) and reports what it finds: whether a coin's structure passed a published checklist 30 seconds after launch, and what happened in the minutes after. It is information about coins' structure. It is not financial, investment, legal or tax advice, and not a recommendation to buy, sell or hold anything. assay shows no prices and makes no predictions. A coin marked "pure" can still lose all of its value, and often does.

Automated, and sometimes wrong

assay's checks are automated and run on incomplete information: it only knows launches since it started, a funding lookup can miss a link between wallets, a metadata host can be down, and a feed can drop a transaction. Findings can be wrong, late or missing, and are corrected by adding to the record, not by rewriting it. Do your own research.
