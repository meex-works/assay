The overlay for pump.fun

A small userscript that puts assay's verdict on pump.fun itself: a badge on every coin, and on a coin's page, the checklist and what has happened since launch. It's free, it's open source, and it's read-only.

Install the userscript

Install it

  1. Install a userscript manager for your browser: Tampermonkey (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari) or Violentmonkey (Chrome, Firefox).
  2. Open assay.meex.work/assay.user.js. The manager shows the script and asks to install it. Read it first if you like: it's about 200 lines, with a comment at the top saying everything it does.
  3. Open pump.fun. Coins get a badge once assay has judged them, 30 seconds after launch.

In Chrome, Tampermonkey also needs Allow user scripts switched on in the extension's details page. The manager asks once whether the script may connect to assay.meex.work: that's the only site it talks to.

What you'll see

  • pure or pure · watching: passed every check at 0:30, and is inside its fifteen-minute watch.
  • broke or died: was pure, then wasn't. Hover for when and why.
  • not pure: failed a check at 0:30. Hover for which.
  • dead: fewer than three outside buyers at 0:30, or a market cap under $2.75K.
  • few buyers, judging, not seen: under ten independent buyers; launched less than 30 s ago; or launched before assay started.

Pure-launch toasts. When a coin is posted pure to Assay Office, a green card appears in the top-right corner of any pump.fun page, with Open (this window) and New tab. Toasts fade after 45 seconds unless the pointer is on them, and the userscript menu can turn them off. Coins held back for the comparison set never toast.

On a coin's page, a panel in the corner shows the checklist and the timeline. The minus button collapses it, and it stays collapsed. The userscript manager's menu has Hide assay badges if you only want the panel.

What it doesn't do

  • It never reads or touches your wallet, and never signs anything.
  • It sends assay.meex.work nothing but the mint addresses on the page.
  • It shows no prices, and nothing it shows is a recommendation.

Or the browser extension

The same code as an extension, no userscript manager needed. It's on its way to the Chrome and Firefox stores; until then, Chrome, Edge and Brave can load it by hand:

  1. Download assay-extension.zip and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions, switch on Developer mode, and choose Load unpacked.
  3. Pick the unzipped folder. It asks for access to pump.fun and assay.meex.work only.

The source is on GitHub; the extension is built from it by one script, so the two never differ. See privacy for exactly what it sends.
